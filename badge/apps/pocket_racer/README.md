# Pocket Racer

Race another badge and dash traffic into their lane in this two-player game.

## Usage

Choose **Solo** with Select for an offline single-player race. For optional
multiplayer, connect both badges to the same Wi-Fi network in **Settings**;
one player chooses **Host** with Left and the other chooses **Join** with
Right. Use Left/Right to steer. In multiplayer, press Select to dash through
nearby traffic and send it into the rival's lane. Collect falling power-ups by
steering into them: **B** boosts speed for five seconds, **+** grants an extra
life (up to three), and **S** blocks one crash. Avoid traffic to stay in the
race; Back exits after a multiplayer round. Local Wi-Fi discovery must be
allowed by the network. The best score is saved on each badge.
